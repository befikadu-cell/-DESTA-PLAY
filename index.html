How to Play

Keno

Choose your numbers before betting closes. The server draws the winning numbers. Your result and balance are calculated by the game server.

Bingo

Choose a Bingo stake, select your cartela and place your bet. When numbers are called, mark the numbers that have been called. If you complete a valid winning pattern, press BINGO. The server verifies the claim.

Roulette

Select the available roulette bet. Wait for the round to finish. The server determines the winning result and calculates the result of the bet.

Aviator

Enter your bet and press BET before the flight begins. During the flight, your possible cash-out amount increases with the multiplier. Tap the running amount to cash out before the flight crashes. If the flight crashes before you cash out, the bet is lost according to the server rules.

How to Deposit

Open Wallet and press Deposit. Enter the amount. The payment account information will appear. Complete the payment outside the application. Then paste the receipt link and submit it. The server records the deposit as processing and the administrator verifies the payment before the balance is credited.

How to Withdraw

Open Wallet and press Withdraw. Choose Telegram as the withdrawal method, enter the amount, payment number and account name, then submit the request. Your withdrawal information is sent securely to the administrator for processing. The request remains recorded in your transaction history.
