Withdrawal
Select Withdraw, enter an amount that does not exceed your withdrawable balance, enter your Telebirr number and account name, then confirm the request with your existing account password.

Playthrough
Deposited money is initially restricted from withdrawal. As the player completes qualifying wagers, the server increases the withdrawable portion according to the platform's wagering rule.
