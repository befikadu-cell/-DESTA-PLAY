How to Play

Keno

Select your numbers and confirm your bet. The server draws numbers during the round. Your result depends on the numbers selected and the official game rules.

Bingo

Choose a stake, enter a Cartela number, place your bet and follow the live called numbers. Mark called numbers on your Cartela. When your winning pattern is complete, submit Bingo. The server verifies every claim.

Roulette

Select Red, Black or Zero before betting closes. The wheel produces the official result. The server determines whether your bet wins.

Aviator Flight

Place your bet before the flight starts. During the flight, the multiplier increases. Your live cash-out amount is your bet multiplied by the current multiplier. Tap the running amount to cash out before the flight crashes. If the flight crashes before you cash out, the bet is lost. The server controls the official multiplier and crash result.

Round Timing

Aviator uses a 10-second interval between rounds. Keno, Bingo and Roulette use a 40-second interval between rounds. The server is authoritative for the actual timing.
